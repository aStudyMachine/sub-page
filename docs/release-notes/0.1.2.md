## English

### New

- The context menu can copy a page's vault-relative path or its absolute path.

### Fixed

- The sidebar keeps its expanded and collapsed layout after you close and reopen a vault.
- Switching from another note to a parent page that is already expanded keeps its children open; clicking the current parent page again collapses them.

### Compatibility

- Requires Obsidian 1.15.0 or later on desktop and mobile.

## 简体中文

### 新增

- 右键菜单可以复制页面的相对路径与绝对路径。

### 修复

- 关闭并重新打开笔记库后，侧栏的展开与折叠状态不再丢失。
- 从其他笔记切换到已展开的父页面时保留子页面；再次点击当前父页面才折叠。

### 兼容性

- 需要 Obsidian 1.15.0 及以上，支持桌面和移动端。
